# CQ-ADMIN-2.0 rollout

Source: CHAO_NGO_ADMIN_QA_COPY_PASTE_v2.md, 10 October 2026.

13 versioned forms: onboarding, four case pretests, four case submissions, four posttests. Prior records are retained. These are candidate items; grading is disabled pending advisor review and pilot. Correct-answer proposals are stored only in the private admin repository/database source notes, never the player repository or APIs.

User override: AI companion and private AI PDF remain required for every case. No slides. Other AI conversation links remain optional. Existing full consent notice and approved policy are retained; the short RC text is not substituted for them.

D09/D10 are asked after choosing a case, before its first evidence. Onboarding groups are personal details, science/game experience, AI/learning methods, and field interests. S07–S10 are optional with “ไม่ได้ใช้ AI” fallback. Follow-up 7–14 days is not enabled because no existing follow-up collection channel exists; no messages are sent.

Completed baseline forms are never re-opened by live updates. Already exposed users can record prior exposure in D09. No claim of validated learning gains is made.
